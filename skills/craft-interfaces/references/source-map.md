## Interface Craft library inventory

### Working Knowledge

- **Noticing** — evidence-first observation and attention to detail.
- **Conceptual Range** — explore meaningfully different directions.
- **Conceptual Depth** — refine a chosen direction through intentional passes.
- **Live Tuning** — create a direct feedback loop between values and result.
- **Animations as Proof of Care** — use meaningful motion to demonstrate
  thoughtfulness, capability, and trust.
- **Uncommon Care** — go one useful step beyond the expected stopping point.
- **Separation of Concerns** — evaluate product, interaction, visual, and motion
  questions on their own terms before synthesis.
- **Facets of Quality** — define the attributes that matter for this product.
- **Less, but Better** — remove unresolved or redundant decisions.
- **Recreate Everything** — learn systems by rebuilding and explaining them.
- **Industry Standards** — treat category conventions as an invisible quality
  floor.

These concepts are synthesized in `design-process.md`, `critique.md`, and
`motion.md`.

### Interface Craft

### Make Interfaces Feel Better

The installed skill contributes:

- text wrapping, font smoothing, and tabular figures;
- concentric surfaces, optical alignment, depth, image outlines, and hit areas;
- interruptible state changes, enter/exit behavior, icon swaps, and press
  feedback;
- transition specificity and careful compositor hints.

This package treats numeric values as tunable heuristics rather than universal
laws and places each detail inside product, interaction, and accessibility
context.
